Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about the E-Sports Club, tournaments, membership, and more.

General

The E-Sports Club is a university-based community for gamers of all skill levels. We organize tournaments, training sessions, social events, and competitions for games like Valorant, Mobile Legends, EA Sports FC, and DOTA 2. Our goal is to create a welcoming environment where gamers can compete, connect, and grow together.

The club is open to everyone! Whether you are a university student, alumni, or a member of the public, you are welcome to join. We believe in creating an inclusive community for all gamers. Simply register through our Registration page and start participating in our events and activities.

Currently, membership is free! We are supported by the university and our generous sponsors. However, some special tournaments or events may have a small participation fee to cover prizes and operational costs. These will be clearly communicated during registration.

Tournaments

To participate in tournaments, simply visit our Tournaments page to see upcoming events. Click on the tournament you are interested in and follow the registration instructions. You will need to be a registered club member and may need to form a team or register as an individual player depending on the tournament format.

We currently support tournaments for Valorant, Mobile Legends, EA Sports FC, and DOTA 2. We are always open to adding more games based on member interest. If you have a game in mind, please let us know through our Contact page!

Absolutely! We value member feedback and suggestions. You can use our Contact page to share your ideas for new games, tournaments, training sessions, or any other activities you would like to see. We regularly review suggestions and plan events based on community interest.

Membership

Becoming a member is easy! Simply visit our Registration page, fill in your details, choose your preferred game, and submit the form. You will receive a confirmation message, and your account will be created in our system. You can then log in to access member-only features and information.

Logged-in members can access exclusive content such as team rankings, player statistics, tournament registration, and club announcements. You can also save your preferences, track your tournament history, and receive personalized event reminders. Logging in ensures you stay connected with the community and never miss an update.

Yes, we take your privacy seriously. All data submitted through our registration and login forms is stored securely using local storage in your browser. We do not share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy (coming soon).

Contact & Support

You can reach us through our Contact page by filling out the contact form. You can also connect with us on our social media channels - Instagram, Discord, and YouTube - where we post updates and announcements. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours.

The best way to stay updated is to regularly visit our website, particularly our Homepage and News sections. We also recommend following our social media channels (Instagram, Discord, YouTube) and checking our Schedule page for upcoming events. Registered members also receive email notifications for important announcements.
